{# Theme override: the licence badge, fetched only when it is nearly on screen. Identical to the theme's own z_footer_cc_by_sa.html except for four attributes on the image. It sits in the footer of all 3,958 pages, and without loading="lazy" the browser fetches it during the initial load of every one of them, for a decoration most readers never scroll to. Measured on a fully cached page it was 22 ms, the second largest item on the page, ahead of everything the reader actually came for. width and height are given so reserving its space cannot shift the footer when it does arrive, which is the usual cost of making an image lazy. The theme is a separate package, so this cannot be fixed at the source; it is overridden here the same way z_top_menu.html is. Keep it in step if the theme's version changes. #}
